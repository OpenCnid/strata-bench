## Finding and implementation

The existing frozen-skills activation reader rejected active learned overlays,
but the live broker accepted executable/tool-triggered content in note files.
The new [versioned policy](../operations/native-frozen-notes.md) closes the
declared lexical packaging routes at broker write time and checkpoint staging.
It preserves ordinary prose/implicit advice and source-linked ambiguity auditing;
it does not claim semantic completeness against arbitrary encodings.

New `NativeRetentionPolicy/2` pins `frozen-notes-markdown-no-code/1` and only
accepts frozen-skills. The old schema is unchanged. Live campaign dispatch and
broker authorization refuse a legacy frozen-skills policy; explicitly synthetic
conformance and stopped historical readers remain available and unqualified.
Other arms retain their old schema/behavior. The initial inventory is checked
before policy registration; recovery and normal episode checkpoints recheck
designated notes/packages and keep all costs. Successful writes are atomically
joined to a private classification event. Initial category rejection precedes
CAS writes; any final scope rejection leaves no broker publication.
