## Cross-cutting checks

- **Hidden objective versus design documents:** Correct separation. The copied prompt can disclose the scientific purpose to the design agent; it is explicitly operator/design material (`SPEC_PROMPT.md`:3). Gameplay isolation is an actual filesystem/process/network boundary, excludes the spec/research/scoring names, and includes leak tests (`SPEC_PROMPT.md`:16,67,109; `BUILD_PLAN.md`:60–66). Reading optional research while writing a spec does not authorize mounting it into gameplay workers. No contradiction found.
- **Input-track comparability:** Correct. Primary ordinary-input/pixel clients, declared settings convenience, and separately labeled semantic assistance appear in both documents (`SPEC_PROMPT.md`:65,69,79; `BUILD_PLAN.md`:82–99). Agent-authored macros are explicitly bounded to authorized actions in the build plan. No hidden grant of semantic powers is required.
- **Adaptation versus possessions:** Correct core contrast. Experienced and initial-state processes receive matched fresh conversations, game state, equipment, fixtures, tools, and probe budgets; evaluation state is discarded (`SPEC_PROMPT.md`:71). The build plan makes unseen instances and no feedback into the campaign explicit (`BUILD_PLAN.md`:119), supplies a gain estimand (`BUILD_PLAN.md`:133), and distinguishes descriptive natural setbacks from controlled probes (`BUILD_PLAN.md`:135). A useful small strengthening is to copy the explicit no-probe-feedback and previously unseen-instance conditions into the prompt so “discard afterward” cannot be read as discarding only game saves while retaining probe discoveries.
- **Nested and repeated computational budgets:** Correct policy. Helpers draw from their parent's budget (`SPEC_PROMPT.md`:63); branches, delegates, retries, and practice worlds are all charged (`SPEC_PROMPT.md`:73,98). Fixed aggregate and fixed-per-agent scaling are distinct conditions. Rollback cannot erase consumed cost or preserve unfair future knowledge (`SPEC_PROMPT.md`:77; `BUILD_PLAN.md`:159–161). There is no authorization for separate unreported helper budgets. The generated ledger should distinguish actual repeated calls from duplicate ingestion of one usage event, but its exact deduplication contract is appropriately a specification detail.

## Delivery priority

1. Put the simultaneous-capacity invariant and model-drift validity rule into the copied prompt.
2. Embed the compact first-slice evidence and make held-out probe non-feedback explicit.
3. Preserve the existing trust boundaries, track separation, and shared-budget rules; they already close the tested failure modes.

## Final disposition after revision

Re-read only the two handoff files. **The two required fixes and two strengthening suggestions are resolved; no material regression or new contradiction was found.** The preceding review is retained as a historical record, not a list of outstanding findings.

- Capacity is now explicit in the standalone prompt: reject/queue the complete over-capacity campaign, never silently reduce or time-share shared-world N, and distinguish sequential independent campaigns (`SPEC_PROMPT.md`:79; `BUILD_PLAN.md`:39).
- Detected model-generation drift ends the fixed-model segment, quarantines the uncertain interval, and starts a fresh cohort; unavailable immutable identity disallows unqualified fixed-model attribution (`SPEC_PROMPT.md`:75; `BUILD_PLAN.md`:143).
- The first vertical slice now has compact concrete exit evidence, including native plugin loading, authentic modded input, expert-mode assertion, persistent hotkey repair, private authoritative reporting, and charged helper calls (`SPEC_PROMPT.md`:89). Runtime compatibility remains unproven until these gates pass.
- Probe training separation, non-feedback, matched instances, process disposal, and sealed-result protection are explicit (`SPEC_PROMPT.md`:73; `BUILD_PLAN.md`:121). The additions preserve the possessions-matched adaptation comparison.
- `pixels-input-settings/v1` and explicit reconfiguration segments disclose the settings convenience, preserve ordinary-input activation and campaign continuity, charge repair resources, prohibit privileged in-play fixtures/protected settings, and match keymaps across cognitive probes (`SPEC_PROMPT.md`:85–87; `BUILD_PLAN.md`:99–101). These clarify the existing primary track without granting semantic mod actions. No material contradiction was found.
